All 7 geometrically possible bedroom layouts

Room: 125" x 134" · scale 1" = 3px · every valid wall + orientation combination for the bed
Why 7: the bed (78.25"x48.75") is the only piece too large to fit in a leftover corner, so its wall and orientation is the one decision that actually changes the room. Checking every wall in both orientations (headboard-out: 48.75" along the wall / long-side: 78.25" along the wall):
  1. Closet wall (125") — headboard-out only (its longest clear run, 57", is too short for long-side)
  2. Hallway wall (134") — long-side
  3. Hallway wall (134") — headboard-out
  4. Wall opposite the closet (125", no door) — long-side
  5. Wall opposite the closet (125", no door) — headboard-out
  6. Wall opposite the hallway door (134", no door) — long-side
  7. Wall opposite the hallway door (134", no door) — headboard-out
The dresser, desk and shelf can each slide a bit along their wall without changing the character of the room, so one workable, non-overlapping arrangement of them is shown for each bed placement.
